Our Approach

Understanding Design Thinking

A human-centered approach to innovation and problem solving.

🎯

Empathize

Understand the user's needs through observation and engagement.

📌

Define

Frame the right problem to solve based on user insights.

💡

Ideate

Generate a broad range of creative solutions.

🔧

Prototype

Build quick, tangible representations of ideas.

✅

Test

Gather feedback and refine the solution iteratively.

Design Thinking in Action — An Example

The Problem

Elevator Breakdown in a Busy Mall

During peak hours, a mall elevator breaks down, leaving elderly people and those with disabilities stranded on upper floors. Traditional stairs are not accessible for everyone.

→
DT Solution

Inclusive Emergency Evacuation System

Design a portable stair-climbing wheelchair device and a real-time notification system that alerts nearby volunteers and staff to assist affected individuals immediately.
